The Plants

Each profile covers identification, traditional preparation, native location, and the cultures & rituals it belongs to.

Educational & cultural reference. This atlas documents ethnobotanical and anthropological knowledge for learning, research, and cultural appreciation. It is not medical, legal, or safety advice. The legality of these plants varies widely by country, state, and context; many are controlled substances. Traditional use has historically occurred within specific cultural, ceremonial, and guided settings. Do not self-administer without understanding the substantial risks, and always respect the Indigenous communities and living traditions these plants come from.
